Simple Calculator for everyday arithmetic

The Simple mode is available from the calculator icon in the site header. It supports addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, percentages, and grouped expressions. Calculations run in your browser.

How do I open the Simple Calculator?

Click the calculator icon in the header and keep Simple mode selected. Enter values with the on-screen keys or keyboard; Enter evaluates, Backspace deletes, and AC clears the expression.

For business or finance questions, a purpose-built tool can make its inputs and assumptions clearer. Browse all 15 calculators and tools.

Scientific Calculator functions

Scientific mode is a general-purpose calculator for evaluating individual expressions. It supports sin, cos, tan and their inverse functions; base-10 and natural logarithms; square roots; factorials; powers; π and e; parentheses; and DEG/RAD angle modes.

When should I use the Scientific Calculator?

Use it for a one-off expression involving trigonometry, logarithms, roots, powers, or grouped arithmetic. It is not a symbolic algebra system or an engineering-formula solver. For a repeated business metric, use a specialized calculator with clearly stated inputs and assumptions. Read Specialized Calculators vs. Scientific Tools for guidance.
