  <h5>What is the difference between markup and profit margin?</h5>
  <p>Markup is calculated as a percentage of cost price, while profit margin is calculated as a percentage of selling price. A 50% markup equals a 33.3% profit margin. Understanding this distinction prevents costly pricing errors.</p>
  <h5>Mathematical Formulas Utilized:</h5>
  <ul>
    <li><code>Profit Margin = (Selling Price - Cost Price) ÷ Selling Price × 100</code></li>
    <li><code>Markup = (Selling Price - Cost Price) ÷ Cost Price × 100</code></li>
  </ul>
</section>
