Calculator Questions

How do I calculate ROAS?
ROAS is attributed revenue divided by ad spend. For example, $50,000 in attributed revenue from $10,000 in ad spend is 5.0x ROAS. The ROAS Planner also estimates break-even ROAS as 1 divided by the gross-margin fraction. That estimate excludes costs not reflected in the margin, and revenue minus ad spend is not full net profit.
What is a good ROAS for an advertising campaign?
There is no universal “good” ROAS; the target depends on gross margin and other costs. At a 40% gross margin, the simple break-even ROAS is 2.5x (1 ÷ 0.40), before costs not included in that margin. Use the ROAS Planner as an estimate, not a guarantee that a campaign is profitable.
How is compound interest calculated?
For an initial principal, the compound-interest component is A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt), where P is principal, r is the annual rate as a decimal, n is compounding periods per year, and t is years. The Compound Interest Calculator also models an optional monthly contribution and reports total invested, future value, and estimated interest. It assumes a constant rate; it does not display an interactive growth chart.
How does a SIP calculator estimate investment growth?
The SIP Investment Planner uses monthly contributions and an assumed annual return. For a positive monthly rate r and n monthly periods, its maturity estimate is PMT × [((1 + r)^n − 1) ÷ r] × (1 + r), with contributions modeled at the beginning of each period. It is a constant-return projection, not a promised investment result.
How is income tax calculated?
The Income Tax Calculator applies one of two built-in progressive slab schedules, selected by category, to estimate annual and monthly tax, effective rate, and take-home income. It has no jurisdiction or tax-year selector, and the site-wide currency display does not convert the slab thresholds. Check current local rules before relying on the estimate.
How do I calculate profit margin?
Profit is selling price minus cost. Profit margin = (Profit ÷ Selling Price) × 100; markup = (Profit ÷ Cost Price) × 100. For example, a 50% markup on cost equals a 33.3% margin on selling price. The Profit Margin Calculator reports profit, margin, and markup from the values entered.
Can I use FinCalcHub calculators on mobile?
Yes. FinCalcHub Marketing is designed for mobile, tablet, and desktop screens. On supported devices, you may be able to install the site as a Progressive Web App for easier access; network-dependent calculators still require a connection.
Are my calculator inputs stored?
Supported calculator inputs and preferences may be saved in your browser’s localStorage, and some active-tab or current-session state uses sessionStorage. Form values are not sent to FinCalcHub as part of the calculation itself. Analytics and crypto-price requests are separate site requests, and using Share can put calculator values in a URL fragment. See the Privacy Policy for details.
How does the crypto price conversion work?
The Crypto Converter requests reference prices from the site’s same-origin /api/crypto-prices endpoint; the project handler retrieves CoinGecko data. It covers Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana with 30 fiat quote currencies. While the page is active it attempts refreshes about every 30 seconds. A cached or fallback value may be shown when current data is unavailable.
How accurate are the crypto converter’s prices?
When current data is available, the converter uses CoinGecko reference prices. Cached or fallback values may be older or illustrative, and a reference price can differ from a specific exchange’s quote, fees, or execution price. Check your exchange directly before making a trade; the converter is not a trading service.
How do I share a calculator result?
Use the Share control on a supported calculator. The link includes the active calculator and its non-empty, identified input and select values in the URL fragment after #, so the recipient can restore them and recalculate. The fragment is not encrypted; anyone with the link can view the values. Do not share sensitive information.
Can I save a calculation in my browser?
Supported calculator inputs may be saved automatically in browser storage and restored later. On supported calculators, PDF and CSV files are assembled in the browser. XLSX export uses the locally hosted SheetJS file /xlsx.mini.min.js, loaded before export use and also preloaded after a short delay. The CSV action also loads that local file before running the site’s browser-based CSV generator.
What currencies are supported?
The site-wide currency selector supports 30 currencies and uses preset conversion factors to change displayed calculator amounts; it is not a live foreign-exchange feed. Crypto conversion is separate: it uses CoinGecko reference prices for BTC, ETH, and SOL with the same 30 fiat quote currencies when data is available.
Is FinCalcHub free to use?
Yes. FinCalcHub Marketing’s current suite of 15 calculator tools is free to use and does not require account registration.
Does FinCalcHub work offline?
The service worker caches core app assets. Offline access is limited to the Simple and Scientific quick calculators; the other finance and business calculators, crypto-price requests, and information pages require an internet connection.
Does FinCalcHub include standard operational calculators?
Yes. A Simple Calculator and a Scientific Calculator are available from the calculator icon in the site header. Simple mode handles everyday arithmetic; Scientific mode adds functions such as trigonometry, logarithms, roots, powers, and factorials.
Can I perform engineering or trigonometry math on this platform?
The Scientific Calculator supports general arithmetic, sin, cos, tan, inverse trigonometric functions, base-10 and natural logarithms, square roots, factorials, powers, constants π and e, and DEG/RAD angle modes. It evaluates individual expressions in the browser; it is not a symbolic algebra system or an engineering-formula solver.
