👋 About FinCalcHub Marketing

15 browser-based calculators for business, finance, crypto, and everyday math

What Is FinCalcHub?

FinCalcHub Marketing brings 15 browser-based calculators together for marketers, freelancers, SaaS teams, e-commerce sellers, and individuals. The suite covers campaign metrics, business unit economics, personal-finance estimates, tax, crypto-to-fiat pricing, and everyday mathematics. Most calculator formulas run in the browser, and no account is required; the site also uses Google Analytics and retrieves crypto prices through its endpoint. See the Privacy Policy for storage and sharing details.

The suite includes:

  • Marketing metrics — ROAS and A/B conversion-rate estimates
  • Business calculations — SaaS CAC/LTV, cost-and-price margins, per-unit e-commerce estimates, and invoice deductions
  • Personal finance — compound-interest, SIP, savings-goal, and retirement projections
  • Tax estimates — fixed built-in progressive slabs that should be checked against current local rules
  • Crypto conversion — BTC, ETH, and SOL reference prices in supported fiat currencies, using CoinGecko data when available
  • Everyday math — built-in Simple and Scientific calculators

Why We Built This

Financial planning and business decisions benefit from clear assumptions. Whether you're reviewing an ad campaign, projecting retirement savings, or estimating e-commerce profit from entered costs, a calculator can make the arithmetic easier to inspect; verify inputs and omitted costs before relying on a result.

FinCalcHub Marketing was built to make common financial and business formulas easier to use without an account. Results are estimates, so users should review assumptions and verify important decisions.

How It Works

🔒 Browser-Based Calculations

Most calculator formulas run in the browser, and the calculation itself does not require sending form values to FinCalcHub. The main calculator page loads Google Analytics and requests crypto-price data through its same-origin endpoint; the project handler retrieves reference prices from CoinGecko. A share action can place input values in the URL fragment. See the Privacy Policy for details.

📲 Progressive Web App

On supported devices, FinCalcHub can be added to the home screen. The service worker caches the app shell; the Simple and Scientific calculators remain available offline, while the other calculators and information pages require a connection.

💾 Save to Browser

Supported calculator values and preferences may be stored in localStorage and restored on a later visit. Persistent input values have no automatic expiry. Some in-session state is stored in sessionStorage and ends with the session.

🔗 Shareable Calculations

A share link includes the active calculator and its entered values in the URL fragment so the recipient can restore them. The values are not encrypted; anyone with the link can view them. Do not share sensitive information in a calculator link.

Simple Calculator for everyday arithmetic

The Simple mode is available from the calculator icon in the site header. It supports addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, parentheses, and percentage operations; calculations run in the browser.

How do I open the Simple Calculator?

Click the calculator icon in the header and keep Simple mode selected. Enter values with the on-screen keys or keyboard; Enter evaluates, Backspace deletes, and AC clears the expression.

Scientific Calculator functions

Scientific mode adds general-purpose trigonometric and inverse-trigonometric functions, base-10 and natural logarithms, square roots, factorials, powers, π and e constants, parentheses, and a DEG/RAD angle toggle. It is a general arithmetic tool, not a symbolic algebra or engineering-formula solver.

When should I use the Scientific Calculator?

Use it for one-off calculations involving trigonometry, logarithms, roots, powers, or grouped expressions. For a repeated business formula, a purpose-built calculator can make its inputs and assumptions easier to review; see the guide to specialized and scientific calculators.
