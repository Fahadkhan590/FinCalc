Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

Zero Local Data Retainment

At FinCalc.ai, user privacy is our highest architectural priority. We operate under a strict client-side zero data retainment policy.

All financial calculations, cryptocurrency input quantities, mortgage loan parameters, interest rates, CPM campaign budgets, and expected conversion benchmarks entered into our single-page web application are processed entirely within your browser's local memory (RAM).

🔒
No Database Storage: FinCalc.ai does not maintain backend database clusters or logging servers to capture, transmit, or archive your personal calculation inputs or financial estimates. Once you close or refresh your browser tab, all inputted parameters are permanently wiped from browser memory.
2

Cookies & Google AdSense Analytics

To keep FinCalc.ai 100% free for worldwide users, we partner with third-party programmatic advertising networks, specifically Google AdSense, to display contextually relevant banner advertisements.

Third-party programmatic advertising vendors, including Google, utilize DART cookies and standard web tracking technologies to serve advertisements based on your prior visits to FinCalc.ai and other websites across the internet.

  • Personalized Advertising: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve targeted advertisements to users based on user location, internet browser history, and demographic indicators.
  • User Opt-Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's official Ads Settings page. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor tracking cookies by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • API Analytics: When fetching real-time cryptocurrency exchange rates, anonymized HTTP requests are made to CoinGecko public API endpoints. No personally identifiable information (PII) is transmitted during these requests.
